# Blind Audition Pipeline + Cold Outreach Campaign

## Target qualification (before any outreach)

Qualify IN if at least two hold:
- Runs its own mock series (not just reselling a national series) — they own the
  content problem.
- Batch size 100+ students, or online mentor with 20k+ engaged following.
- Has a named VARC faculty/head (the audition needs an evaluator who cares).
- Charges premium fees (₹40k+ course) — they sell quality, so quality proof lands.

Qualify OUT: franchisees of national chains (no content authority), free-content
YouTubers (no budget), anyone demanding copyright assignment on first call.

## The five-stage pipeline

**Stage 0 → 1 conversion trigger is always the free audit, never a demo.**

### Stage 1 — The Hook: free Structural Repetition Audit
They send 6–10 RCs from their current mock series. Run the fingerprinter
(`vet` on each, against a temporary corpus of just their sets). Return a 2-page
report: skeleton reuse count, commitment-curve correlations between their sets,
answer-key letter/length patterns, same-author stylometric flags.
- Cost to us ≈ ₹0 (local compute + Haiku passes).
- Psychology: we never claim "we're better" — we prove *their current content is
  learnable*. Skepticism about AI becomes irrelevant; the numbers are about them.
- Every audit ends with one line: "This is what your serious repeaters have
  already unconsciously learned."

### Stage 2 — The Blind Audition
5-passage pack: 3 ours (different families, different personas, different
endings), 1 strong human-written set, 1 vanilla-ChatGPT set. Structured scoring
sheet for their VARC head: (a) rank overall quality, (b) mark which are
AI-generated, (c) mark any pair that "feels like the same author."
- **Pre-commit in writing**: "If your VARC head identifies our 3 at better than
  chance, we walk away and you keep the sets."
- Marginal cost: 3 elite sets ≈ ₹40–80. Run as many auditions as the pipeline holds.

### Stage 3 — The Reveal
After they score, send the per-RC dossier: blueprint summary (family, persona,
ending — plain-language names), the 9-channel novelty report, blind-solver and
judge logs. The message: "You just experienced the output; this is the governance
behind every set." Attach the 9-channel explainer PDF.

### Stage 4 — Paid Pilot
One complete mock VARC section (4 sets, difficulty-curved): ₹5,000–8,000,
credited against the first retainer month if signed within 30 days. Paid pilots
filter tire-kickers and create internal budget precedent.

### Stage 5 — Close
Retainer (tier per pricing-packaging.md) + 12-month category+city exclusivity
option + MSA (license not assignment; no-redistribution clause; see ops file).

## Funnel math (plan against these ratios)

20 qualified outreach → 8 audit acceptances → 5 auditions → 3 paid pilots →
2 signed retainers. Cost per signed retainer: under ₹500 API + your hours.
To reach 5 retainers: ~50–60 qualified targets over 60–75 days.
