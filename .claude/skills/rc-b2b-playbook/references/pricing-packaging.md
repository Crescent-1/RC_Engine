## The three tiers

### Tier 1 — Practice Bench · ₹15,000–20,000/month
For: small coachings (<150 students), independent online VARC mentors.
- 24 sets/month, medium+hard tier mix (2–3 elite).
- Drawn from the shared component pool (no exclusivity).
- Monthly one-page novelty summary (corpus health extract).
- Standard delivery: weekly export batch, .txt/.docx.
- Purpose in the ladder: replaces a part-time content writer at comparable price
  with better consistency; upgrade path to Mock Vault at mock season.

### Tier 2 — Mock Vault (flagship) · ₹40,000–55,000/month
For: boutique CAT coachings building their own mock series (150–600 students).
- 40 sets/month = one complete 10-mock CAT VARC series per month
  (4 RCs/mock — matches CAT's 4-RC VARC section).
- Difficulty curves designed per mock (topology + tier mapping), not random.
- **Structural exclusivity**: no set shares an argument-family skeleton with any
  other client's sets (combo-hash disjointness, auditable on demand).
- Per-mock **Novelty Certificate**: 1-page audit extract per mock (9-channel
  scores vs. their own corpus).
- **Trap-map teaching notes**: answer explanations name the designed misreading —
  faculty debrief material included free (this is the faculty-adoption lever).
- 72-hour solver-dispute / correction SLA.
- Quarterly corpus health report (their students' exposure hygiene).

### Tier 3 — Platform Feed · ₹75,000–1,25,000/month
For: test platforms, LMS products, large online-mock businesses.
- 100–150 sets/month, CSV/JSON delivery mapped to their schema.
- **Per-student novelty guarantee**: no student sees the same argument family
  twice within a mock series, no (family × ending) pair ever — sold as a product
  feature they can market ("no two RCs you receive share an argumentative skeleton").
- Difficulty metadata per slot for adaptive engines.
- Co-branded audit trail (their brand on the novelty certificates).
- Requires: proprietorship/OPC entity, GST registration (see ops file).

## Billing & negotiation guardrails (Indian edu market)

- **Quarterly advance billing** is the default. Edu sector pays late; never extend
  more than one month of credit. 10% discount for annual prepay.
- Clients will deduct **TDS u/s 194J (10%)** — quote prices gross of TDS.
- Quote per-mock equivalents in negotiations: Mock Vault at ₹45k = ₹4,500 per
  complete mock VARC section — vs. what one good VARC faculty-day costs them.
- Floor prices: never below ₹625/set (Practice Bench floor). Walk away below it —
  cheap clients cost the most review time.
- Pilot pricing: one mock section ₹5,000–8,000, **credited against first retainer
  month** if they convert within 30 days.
- Founding client: grandfather at ₹18–20k/mo in exchange for written testimonial,
  reference calls, and case-study rights (sets delivered, dispute rate, novelty
  stats). Do not reprice them to rack rate; they are the proof asset.
