# Career Roadmap: July 2026 → July 2027 (CAT-First)

**For:** Ansh Upadhyay · Mumbai
**Decision made:** CAT 99%ile is the single priority until Nov 29, 2026. Living with parents → burn is minimal, no cash cliff. Only ongoing work: RC contract, capped at 10 hrs/week. Everything else starts Dec 1. MBA only if top-5 (IIM A/B/C/L, FMS-tier). Income target: ₹2–3L/mo by mid-2027.

---

## Phase 1 — Jul 14 → Nov 29, 2026: CAT only (138 days)

You have your own prep plan — this roadmap doesn't duplicate it. It only holds the guardrails:

**Hard deadlines**

- **Aug 1 – Sep 20:** CAT registration window at iimcat.ac.in. Register in the first week — do not let this ride.
- **Nov 29:** exam day, three slots.

**Percentile milestones (trajectory check, not judgment)**

- End Aug: full mocks begun, ≥85%ile overall
- End Sep: ≥92–95%ile, no section below 85
- End Oct: ≥97%ile at least once; weakest section identified and drilled
- Nov: consolidation and taper — no new material after Nov 15

If a milestone slips, the response is to fix *how* you're prepping (mock analysis depth, section time strategy, weak-area drills) — never to add more hours past the point of diminishing returns, and never to borrow hours from sleep.

**Your structural edge:** you built a machine that deconstructs VARC — argument skeletons, distractor logic, trap taxonomy. Use your own engine's trap-maps as VARC revision material; that's revision time nobody else in the country has. Guard QA and DILR time accordingly, since that's where engineers' attempts are usually decided.

**The RC contract (10 hrs/week, unchanged workload)**

- One exception to "nothing but CAT": send **one repricing email** to the institute. You deliver ~48 sets/month for ₹10k (~₹208/set); even generic freelance RC writers charge ₹1,500–3,000/set. Ask ₹40–50k/mo for the same delivery. One conversation, same 10 hours, 4–5x the income. If they refuse, keep it at ₹10k — it's portfolio proof and pocket money; renegotiate again in December from a stronger position.
- No new features, no new clients, no pitching until December. Delivery only.

**Explicitly banned until Nov 29:** freelance platforms, job applications, blog posts, engine upgrades, new-client pitches, "just exploring" anything. Every one of these is a percentile leak.

## Phase 2 — Dec 1, 2026 → Mar 2027: the income sprint

Starts **Dec 1 regardless of how the exam felt** — perceived CAT performance predicts nothing, and offers-in-hand before results day (early Jan) is leverage in both directions.

**December (job-hunt sprint + quick assets)**

- Week 1–2: portfolio/LinkedIn refresh; publish one technical write-up on the 9-channel novelty auditor (your inbound magnet and interview anchor — highest-ROI single item in this plan); containerize + deploy the RC engine behind an API on AWS/GCP (fills your one real skill gap).
- Week 2 onward: 30+ applications. Targets in pay order: **GCCs and product companies (₹35–50 LPA)** → **funded AI startups (₹25–40 LPA + equity)** → **remote US contracting ($2.5–4.5k/mo)**. Skip IT services — they cap below your target.
- Positioning, every conversation: *banking-grade data engineering (EY/HSBC) + production LLM systems with hard cost governance + evaluation methodology.* Almost nobody has all three. The RC engine is the case study.
- Interview prep in parallel: ML system design + Python DSA refresh — 3 focused weeks, not 3 months.
- Track C restart: repricing round 2 with current client; pitch 5–8 institutes (Dec–Jan is when they plan next-cycle content) with the free structural-audit hook.

**January 2027 — results and the fork**

- **Top-5 call:** prep WAT/PI seriously — but keep interviewing. An offer in hand makes the MBA a choice, not an escape.
- **No top-5 call:** zero deliberation, zero mourning — you're already a month into the job sprint. Full speed.

**February–March 2027**

- Sign the best offer (**target ₹25–40 LPA = ₹2.1–3.3L/mo**) or, if remote contracting is converting faster, stack contracts to the same number.
- **Mar 31 gate for Passage Works:** ≥₹75k/mo across clients → keep it alive on ≤5 hrs/week (delivery is automated). Below that → park it, keep the single best client, revisit in 12 months from financial safety. It remains the best portfolio asset you own either way.

**The MBA decision, if it arrives (Mar–Apr):** top-5 MBA ≈ ₹25–35L fees + 2 years of forgone ₹30 LPA income ≈ **₹85L–1Cr real cost**. Worth it for a deliberate pivot into consulting/finance/leadership; not obviously worth it to earn what the AI market will already pay you. Decide with offers on the table.
