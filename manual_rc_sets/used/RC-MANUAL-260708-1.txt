[PASSAGE]

Every teacher of writing I know repeats the same complaint, and I used to repeat it too. Students no longer hold a thought in the head. They externalize everything: notes, outlines, phones, the search bar. The muscle of memory, we say, therefore wastes. We call this offloading, and we say it as a diagnosis, the way one names a deficiency. The mind, on this account, has grown lazy because it has grown assisted, and the assistance is the wound.

I have come to think the complaint mistakes the symptom for the disease, and I want to say why with some care. Offloading is not the failure of a memory that should have done the work. It is what a well-run memory does when the environment becomes reliable enough to trust. Ask what decision the lament would change. If a student who never memorizes a phone number is failing, then curing him means restoring a habit that buys nothing. The number is on the device, retrievable in less time than recall would take. The head that declines to store what the world stores for it is not weaker. It is doing the arithmetic correctly.

The evidence for this is duller than the alarm suggests. When people expect information to remain available, they remember where it lives rather than what it says. When they expect it to vanish, they encode the content instead. The mechanism tracks the reliability of the store, not the strength of the mind. That is why the same person offloads a grocery list and memorizes a lover's moods. Nothing has decayed. A system that once had only its own tissue to write on now has cheaper media nearby, and it allocates accordingly, the way any careful accountant moves what he can off the expensive ledger.

None of this means the worry is empty. It means the worry was misfiled. The cost is real, but it sits elsewhere. Not in a wasting faculty but in a dependency, so that when the reliable store fails, the person who trusted it correctly is left holding nothing. That is a claim about the fragility of infrastructure, not the decline of minds, and the two demand different remedies. The faculty is intact and doing its job. What we have built around it is what cannot be trusted to stay.
