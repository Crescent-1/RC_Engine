[PASSAGE]

A society does not discover its heroes so much as ratify them, and the ratification is conducted in a vocabulary nobody has been asked to approve. Two parties presented themselves during the pandemic, each with its credentials in order. The first held that nurses and physicians who reported to contaminated wards, in insufficient gowns, with no prospect of relief, had earned the honorific outright; the template offered was Omaha Beach, the 116th Infantry Regiment walking into fire on the morning of June 6, 1944, and the argument was that fire is fire whatever its chemistry. The second party, no less courteous, observed that a shift is a shift. One clocks in. One is compensated, insured, and scheduled. To confer on paid attendance the laurel reserved for men who crossed a beach knowing the arithmetic is, this party suggested, an act of generosity so lavish it impoverishes the recipient. Both sides conducted themselves as though the matter were a question of measurement — as though there existed some agreed threshold of heroism, and the only remaining business were to determine whether the wards cleared it. They were not measuring against the same mark. Each had brought its own, unpacked it without ceremony, and proceeded.

The quarrel did not stay in the seminar. Mattel produced a #ThankYouHeroes toy line of nurses, physicians, EMTs and delivery workers. Signs went into windows. At seven each evening the quarantined populace clanged pots and pans through the streets in honor of essential workers. Millions of people were thereby assenting, nightly, to a proposition most of them had never stated. Some of the honored resented it. The volume of an endorsement is not an argument for its accuracy, and a word applauded on schedule can still be the wrong word; what the pots established was not the meaning but the cost of leaving it unsettled.

Consider a nurse who chose none of it. She entered the profession in a quiet year, signed a contract, took on a code of professional duty, and then found the year had changed around her while the obligations stayed where they were. Thomas Carlyle, lecturing on heroes in the mid-nineteenth century, treated the visible world as the embodiment of Thoughts that dwelt in Great Men; the hero, on that account, alters the trajectory of mankind and changes the soul of humanity. Such a figure acts from outside any institution's claim on him — that is nearly the point of him. The nurse acts from inside one. Both are at risk; only one is at liberty. So the word has been assigned two incompatible offices, marking exceptional choice in the one case and exceptional endurance in the other, and it cannot discharge both in a single sentence.

Which suggests that the tribunal was convened on the wrong charge. Nobody needed to know whether the wards cleared a bar. What the clanging pots were really adjudicating, without knowing it, was a prior preference: whether a public means to honor the act freely undertaken or the post not abandoned. That choice is the thing being made, and it is being made in a word too worn to register that it is making it.
