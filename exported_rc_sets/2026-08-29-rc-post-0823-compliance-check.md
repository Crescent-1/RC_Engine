## Cross-cutting notes

- **Length failures are all in one direction.** Four papers miss the band, all over, by 1, 2, 3 and 13 words. Three are trivially fixable. This inverts the pattern noted in `_TRIAGE.txt`, where the length failures were 7-of-8 OpenAI sets running short.
- **The assumption-stem template has recurred.** It appeared twice in the previous batch (0052 Q4, 0056 Q8) and once here (0047 Q7). If the generator has a question-type list, this stem is on it and should come off.
