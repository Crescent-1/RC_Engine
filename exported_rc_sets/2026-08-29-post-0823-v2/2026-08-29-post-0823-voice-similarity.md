# Voice Similarity — the 9 post-0823 papers

## Verdict

They sound like one writer. Not "a shared register" — one writer, working from one template, with a recurring vocabulary. Read singly each is good. Read consecutively, a candidate who does three of them will start predicting the fourth.

This matters here because the parent folder is named `post_housevoice_fix`. Whatever that fix was, it did not take.

---

## The shared skeleton

All nine execute the same seven moves in the same order.

| # | Move | Present in |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold open on a physical object with an exact count | 7 / 9 |
| 2 | Stipulate a definition ("as the term will be used here") | 6 / 9 |
| 3 | Steelman the rival reading, generously and explicitly | 9 / 9 |
| 4 | Turn on one overlooked datum — usually timing or dating | 9 / 9 |
| 5 | Machine / plumbing / architecture metaphor for the argument | 8 / 9 |
| 6 | Enumerate rival framings, show each fails, decline to pick | 9 / 9 |
| 7 | Close by refusing resolution and naming the cost | 9 / 9 |

**Move 3, verbatim across the set:** "The objection arrives promptly and it deserves its best day" (0044) · "The most careful version of that objection runs roughly so… Given full force, it has weight" (0047) · "The obvious reading is continuity, and it is a good reading" (0067-hard) · "The case for the store, though, survives closer reading and is strengthened by it" (0068) · "for reasons that were not silly" (0065) · "an argument with the considerable advantage of being written down as it happened" (0064) · "I would not argue against either label" (0046) · "it would be careless to treat every such protocol as ornament" (0047).

**Move 7, verbatim:** "The file cannot pay it" (0066) · "The cost of saying so is…" (0068) · "What it has not yet paid for is the aftermath" (0065) · "It will not stay affordable" (0044) · "That accounting belongs to auditors, not to historians" (0066) · "They succeed at something else" (0047).

## The single clearest tell: "survives"

The word appears in **7 of 9** passages, and in four of them it does *identical structural work* — the qualified-survival verdict, in the final movement:

- "So the definition that **survives** is the narrow one." (0066)
- "So the Global South **survives** as a working vocabulary." (0044)
- "The verdict, then, **survives** — but on narrower ground than the opening resemblance suggested." (0046)
- "The scientist's confident claim **survives** in modified form." (0067-elite)

Four papers reaching the same verdict with the same verb in the same position. "Nobody" appears in 7 of 9, "nothing" in 7, "cannot" in 6.

## Sentence rhythm

Every paper runs long analytic sentences broken by bare declarative fragments used as verdicts. The fragment rate ranges 10–35% of sentences; no paper is without them.

- 0064: "The vote took four minutes." / "Projected risk: negligible." / "Every step is dated." / "Every step has a signature." / "The paramedics have no memos." / "They have run sheets."
- 0067-hard: "The petitions scroll." / "The Mass proceeds." / "Both readings live on content." / "The timestamps live elsewhere." / "Nobody read them."
- 0047: "Sincerity is not decoration." / "Refusal can be mere obstinacy." / "An offence is named." / "A speaker is positioned before witnesses."
- 0065: "Type must hold still." / "Stability was the load path." / "None of it established stability."
- 0046: "Two conditions, both required." / "Press a case." / "Not a continuation. Not a reunion."

The paired-negation cadence ("Not a continuation. Not a reunion." / "not prayer and not liturgy but…" / "Not secrecy imposed on civic life, but…" / "Ignition, not infection.") recurs across the set and across the earlier batch too.

## Openings and closings

**Openings** — seven of nine are the same gambit, a physical object plus a number:

> A microphone stand in a hotel conference room, adjusted twice… · The file is thin: eleven pages, a carbon of a checklist… · A quarto notebook catalogued in a county record office… · The vote took four minutes. · A couple has spent eleven months and most of a savings account… · A parish keeps stranger records than it knows. · What earns the name spinoff?

Only 0065 and 0044 open on abstraction — and 0044 opens with "Let me fix what X will mean here", which is itself the template's move 2 hoisted to the front.

**Closings** — all nine end on withheld resolution. None reaches a verdict; each names what the withholding costs.

## Reader-directed imperatives

A stage direction telling the reader where to look, in 7 of 9: "Watch the relocation actually operate" · "Press a case" · "Return to the kitchen" · "Suppose the inspection consisted of…" · "But trace the sequence" · "Set it beside Malawi" · "Set against that:" · "Sort the logs by time" · "Notice what those three exemplars have in common" · "Consider the case that presses hardest".

---

## The narrator

The same figure recurs: a professional who reports having been wrong, with a date attached.

- "I sat in the back row of that room in the spring of 2015 as a stringer…" then "for a long stretch of reporting I assumed it with them" (0064)
- "Those of us who spent years arguing that liturgical practice survives its media… were handed, in that folder, a document we could not read as easily as we expected" (0067-hard)
- "I want to be exact about how strong that consensus was" (0065)
- "I want the word to do real work, so I fix it narrowly" (0046)

The earlier batch has the same man: "For about eleven years, professionally, I built them" (0059) · "I abandoned my earlier reading in 2014" (0039) · "I spent two years treating both files as evidence about causation" (0064).

---

## What this is not

The *subjects* are well spread — ergonomics, sunk cost, a Victorian notebook, an ambulance reroute, a fire inspection, livestreamed Mass, COP28, a TV spinoff, public apology. Nobody is repeating topics. The passages are also individually well made; the problem is not quality.

The similarity is entirely at the level of **argumentative architecture and sentence music**, which is exactly the level a candidate sitting four RCs in one sitting will feel.

---

## What would actually break it

Ranked by how much variety each buys:

1. **Vary the argumentative shape, not the topic.** Every one of these nine is *adjudication-refused*: rival readings, each fails, verdict withheld, cost named. Missing entirely: straight exposition; chronological narrative; a passage that actually argues one side and wins; a passage with a thesis in the first sentence and support after; comparison without adjudication.
2. **Ban the withheld ending in half the set.** The refusal to resolve is the strongest single marker, and it is 9 of 9.
3. **Kill the fragment-as-verdict habit in a subset.** Some passages should run on continuous prose with no one-clause sentences at all.
4. **Vary the opening gambit.** The object-plus-number cold open is 7 of 9.
5. **Retire the recurring lexicon.** "survives", "nobody", "load-bearing", "cost/price/pay", "hold still", "the whole X can be traced".
6. **Vary the narrator.** Drop the first-person reformed-professional in favour of an impersonal register in some papers.

A generator-level fix is probably needed: if all nine come from one prompt scaffold, no amount of per-paper editing will separate them, because the scaffold *is* the similarity.
