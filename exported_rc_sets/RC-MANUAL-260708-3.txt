[PASSAGE]

When anthropologists began studying expertise itself — how diagnostic skill is transmitted among, say, avalanche forecasters or wine graders — a difficulty announced itself before the first field note was filed. We were proposing to become experts in expertise. Whatever we learned about how the studied practitioners came to trust their own judgments would, if it held, apply to us as we came to trust ours. The instrument we were calibrating was the same instrument doing the calibrating.

Take the standard finding first, because it is genuinely robust: across most of the trades we followed, practitioners could not articulate the grounds of their best calls. The avalanche forecaster feels the slope is wrong; pressed, she supplies a rationalization that her own logbook, cross-checked against outcomes, does not support. What we know here is fairly secure — the reconstructions are post hoc. How we know it is worth pausing on: we know it because our transcripts of practitioners' explanations diverge from their recorded decisions, a method that assumes the transcript is the better record.

That assumption is where the recursion enters. For the same skepticism turns, without special pleading, on the fieldworker. When I judge that a forecaster's stated reason is a rationalization, I too am reading a slope — the interview — and issuing a confident call whose grounds I would, if pressed, reconstruct after the fact. Some of my most assured interpretations, the ones I could defend at length, are on this account exactly the ones I should trust least, since fluency of defense is what we learned to discount in the people we studied. The finding, applied inward, does not weaken; it sharpens, and it sharpens against its holder.

One is tempted here to resolve the loop by ranking the levels — to say the analyst's skepticism operates one storey above the practitioner's intuition and is therefore exempt, the way a proofreader need not doubt the alphabet she checks against. But the temptation trades on a difference the evidence does not license: nothing in what we observed marks the interpreter's cognition as a different kind from the forecaster's, only as differently aimed. The storeys are the same material. To claim the auditor's floor is load-bearing in a way the audited floor is not would be to smuggle back the very exemption the study was convened to test.

So the honest report ends without the summary its form seems to promise. I could state a position — that all expert confidence, mine included, is post-hoc narration — but stating it with the assurance a conclusion requires would perform precisely the fluency the finding tells me to distrust, and stating its opposite would do the same. What can be set down is narrower: that the method here has not yet found the place to stand from which it could certify its own results, and that to announce a verdict now, while the instrument still reads its own reflection, would be to mistake the warmth of a well-turned ending for the coldness of a checked one.
