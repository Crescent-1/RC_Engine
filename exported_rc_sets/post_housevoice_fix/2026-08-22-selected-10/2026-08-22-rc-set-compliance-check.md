## Failures in detail

### RC-ELITE-260822-0061 — two problems

**R1: 565 words** — 15 over the ceiling. The whole first paragraph is a 215-word block; trimming the "municipal tradition / free-speech tradition / network culture" taxonomy sentence would bring it inside.

**R2: Q4 and Q8 are the same question.** Q4 asks the purpose of the deaf student's example; answer (B) = "protecting inconspicuousness can itself make an access user conspicuous." Q8 asks *why* she becomes conspicuous; answer (A) = "her access-related upright phone resembles prohibited filming and prompts staff scrutiny." One is the other restated. Replace one — the passage has an unused angle in the security guard's classification problem.

**Minor:** Q1 (A) vs (B) is a thin discrimination. (B) "show how one proposed restriction produces arguments about access, privacy, and enforcement" is a defensible answer to "why does the author include these accounts"; the key rejects it as *level_confusion*, which is a fine distinction to hang a mark on.

### RC-HARD-260821-0052 — assumption question

**R4: Q4 is an assumption question.** "Which one of the following would best **license the inference** the author draws from the three failed trials?" — correct answer (C) "Every long-established craft name can be shown to designate the fixed object that practitioners work upon" is a textbook sufficient assumption (the universal that makes the paragraph-4 leap valid). The key's own rationale says it: "The paragraph-4 leap **requires** that naming-by-fixed-object is general." Rewrite as a strengthen ("would most support") with an evidential rather than universal answer, or swap the question type.

**Minor:** Q7 has two defensible answers. (B) "supply the counter-move that overturns the programming analogy after it has been generously stated" and (C) "locate the precise feature whose absence will later be generalised" are both authorial-purpose readings; the key separates them on a distinction most solvers won't see.

### RC-HARD-260821-0056 — assumption question

**R4: Q8 uses the identical template.** "Which one of the following would best license the inference the author draws from the abolition of the statutes?" Milder than 0052 — the correct answer (C) functions as evidence rather than a bridging universal — but the stem is assumption-family phrasing and reads as one. Since 0052 carries the same stem, both papers in the same set share a question form that shouldn't be there at all.
