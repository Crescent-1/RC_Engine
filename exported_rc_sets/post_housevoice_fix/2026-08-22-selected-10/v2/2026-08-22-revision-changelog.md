## Question replacements

Each replacement introduces a CAT question type the paper did not already carry, and each comes with full elimination logic in the existing tag vocabulary.

### RC-ELITE-260822-0061 — Q8 replaced
**Out:** "why does the deaf student become conspicuous" — a restatement of Q4, which already tested the same point as the purpose of her example.
**In:** **Central idea** (the paper had no main-idea question at all). Answer **(C)**: entry to a public room need not carry consent to circulation, and no settled rule can secure that distinction — the closing paragraph's "surviving proposition." Distractors: outright prohibition (moderate_extremism), open-door-vs-protection as a forced choice (false_symmetry, the border-guard misreading), drafting failure (premature_closure).

### RC-HARD-260821-0052 — Q4 replaced, Q7 (B) sharpened
**Out:** "which would best license the inference…" — a sufficient-assumption question; its own key rationale said the leap "requires" the answer.
**In:** **Application / analogous situation** (absent from the paper). Answer **(C)**: a cartographer whose skill stops accumulating because the coastline is redrawn each season — the correlate will not hold still, which is the sediment of para 3. Distractors: stable object with an inadequate instrument (category_slip), a quality dispute borrowing translation (adjacent_answer), a craft ending because its employment ends (missing_link).
**Also:** Q7 (B) was too defensible against the correct (C). Rewritten as "To close the programming question for good, so that the essay is free to move on…" — a structural-only reading that para 4 falsifies by reopening the compiler as the first of three correlates. Key tag unchanged (level_confusion), rationale updated.

### RC-HARD-260821-0056 — Q8 replaced
**Out:** "which would best license the inference…" — the same assumption-family stem as 0052's Q4.
**In:** **Vocabulary-in-context** (absent from the paper) on "Markets are not referees. They are the game." Answer **(A)**: market competition is the mechanism generating the escalation and so cannot also restrain it. Distractors: voluntary rules (adjacent_answer), markets as successor to the magistrate (stage_misattribution — para 3 says no institution inherited the job), a contest with settled winners (phrase_misuse — para 2 says the winner has merely reached the next round's starting line).

### RC-MEDIUM-260822-0034 — Q3 and Q5 replaced, Q7 (C) reworded
**Out (Q3):** the "complicate the asymmetry" weakener, whose answer turned on the same institutional-force sentence that Q8 asks about directly — Q8 handed the solver Q3's key. This is the `solver_dispute` the manifest flagged; both keys were correct, the fault was redundancy.
**In (Q3):** **Inference.** Answer **(C)**: a compositor using standard usage on a form is accepting one loss to avoid another, not conceding the manual's correctness (para 4: the choice is between two goods, not between correct and incorrect). Trap (D) has the linguists "costing" the choice — para 4 says they "declined, on principle, to say what it cost anyone."
**Out (Q5):** "the passage as a whole is best understood as an attempt to" — a second main-point question duplicating Q1.
**In (Q5):** **Author's attitude/tone** (absent from the paper). Answer **(B)**: admiring of its architecture while unpersuaded that it accounts for the people who speak — para 1's double "One admires."
**Also (Q7):** option (C) said the passage "endorses" the speakers' distrust; the text says they "**may** be right to distrust." Reworded to "granting that their distrust of outside counsel may be warranted." Key rationale updated to match.

### RC-MEDIUM-260822-0037 — Q6 and Q8 replaced
**Out (Q6):** "the value of publishing the school's archive" — a detail restatement of Q3, which already tested that example's function.
**In (Q6):** **Application / analogous case** (absent from the paper). Answer **(B)**: a hospital that admits exclusion, opens its records, then cedes admissions authority — acknowledgment joined to the changes its own account of the past makes due. Distractors: apology plus ceremony (overqualification), a single compensation rate (scope_inflation, against the watchmaker analogy), withholding apology pending full research (premature_closure, against "difficulty is a bad solvent").

**In (Q8):** **EXCEPT-detail** (absent from the paper). Answer **(D)**, the unsupported one: that a carefully drafted apology discharges the duty. The other three are lifted from paras 1, 2 and 4.

### RC-MEDIUM-260822-0039 — Q7 replaced, Q6 stem reworded

**In (Q7):** **Application / parallel situation** (absent from the paper).
