## How I judged similarity

Score and compliance F1 were secondary. The screen I actually applied was **argumentative flow** — the shape of the move the passage makes — because that is where this corpus repeats itself. Five recurring skeletons run through the 19:

| Skeleton | Sets using it |
|---|---|
| **A. Concede-then-split** — steelman a claim, grant its results, deny its mechanism, refuse the "partly both" compromise, close on a hedge | E-0048, E-0050, E-0056, E-0059, H-0059, M-0035 |
| **B. Displacement diagnosis** — the visible dispute/record is a proxy for something unstated; relocate the real question | E-0053, E-0056, E-0057, M-0039, M-0035 |
| **C. Measurement mismatch** — two parties measure different things; the instrument doesn't reach the thing | M-0033, M-0034, M-0038, E-0048 |
| **D. Inherited category fails** — a label formed under vanished conditions is applied to new ones | H-0052, H-0061, H-0056 |
| **E. Archive/record limits** — what was kept documents the keepers, not the subject | E-0056, E-0057, E-0059, H-0060 |

Two more sameness channels worth naming, because they cut across tiers:
- **The authorial recantation opener** ("I used to believe X / I abandoned my earlier reading") — E-0060, H-0059, M-0035, M-0039, E-0053. I capped this at 2 in the final ten.
- **The house tic** `— no: more precisely,` appears verbatim in E-0057-adjacent prose, H-0060, H-0061, M-0033, M-0034, M-0038, M-0039. Not a selection criterion on its own, but a reliable marker that two sets came off the same beat plan. Worth stripping before ship regardless of which ten you take.

## SELECTED — 10

### Elite (3)

**1. RC-ELITE-260821-0048 — strike contagion / epidemiological modelling**
*8.6 · needs_review · F1 0.74 · novelty 0.504*
The only **quantitative** passage in the corpus: R₀, residuals, curve fitting, an aggregate-to-individual inference error. That register is heavily represented on CAT and entirely absent elsewhere here. Skeleton A, but it is the cleanest and hardest-edged execution of it, and the numeric texture keeps it from reading like its cousins. Q4 and Q8 (weaken / apply-the-account) are genuinely CAT-grade.

**2. RC-ELITE-260821-0053 — the deleted sixteen bars / musicology**
*8.8 · approved · F1 0.886 · novelty 0.59*
Highest-scored set in the corpus and the purest instance of skeleton B. Distinctive because it *awards the case* to one side while denying the dispute was ever about what it appeared to be — a verdict-plus-diagnosis close that nothing else attempts. Q6 and Q7 (position-described-not-endorsed, EXCEPT) are well built.

**3. RC-ELITE-260822-0061 — library filming rule**
*7.6 · needs_review · F1 0.77 · novelty 0.595*
Included primarily on structure. It is the **only polyphonic passage in all 19** — six stakeholders, no authorial thesis-march, a synthesis that refuses to resolve. Every other set is a single voice arguing forward. Lowest scores among my elite picks, and the honest trade: I bought architectural difference with about half a point of polish. Q5/Q6/Q7 are strong; Q1 vs Q2 is a slightly tight purpose/structure split worth a second read before ship.

### Hard (3)

**4. RC-HARD-260821-0052 — "Prompt Engineer / Content Strategist"**
*8.6 · approved · **F1 1.0** · novelty 0.585*
Perfect compliance and the best pure-reasoning passage in the set. Three serial trials each fail for a different reason, then the reveal that craft categories name the fixed object, not the activity. The closing redirect (which of our fixed objects are still sitting where we left them) is exactly the CAT "most strongly implied by the ending" target. Take this one as-is.

**5. RC-HARD-260821-0056 — sumptuary law**
*8.6 · needs_review · F1 0.925 · novelty 0.547*
Ridicule → full reversal → a named absence with no remedy proposed. The "we were right to burn the instrument, the loss is real and permanent" close is unlike anything else here — no other set ends by conceding an unfixable loss. Historical sweep gives it a period register the corpus otherwise lacks. Hirsch citation is load-bearing but not decorative.

**6. RC-HARD-260822-0059 — Marguerite's palm on the feed valve**
*8.4 · approved · F1 0.837 · novelty 0.546*
First-person field narrative, and the strongest *scene* in the corpus. The disciplined narrowing ("I cannot demonstrate that her hand caused those three decades; I can demonstrate...") is a rare and genuinely testable authorial move — Q7 exists only because of it. One of my two permitted recantation openers.

### Medium (4)

**7. RC-MEDIUM-260822-0033 — when a friendship ends**
*8.6 · needs_review · F1 0.85 · novelty 0.487*
The cracked-vs-shattered lens pivot from quantity to category, then the terminal claim that the crossing is not merely unmeasured but **unmeasurable** because the quantity doesn't exist prior to someone's reading. That conclusion is unique in the set. Lowest novelty score of the ten (0.487) but the lowest-similarity *argument*, which is what matters here.

**8. RC-MEDIUM-260822-0034 — the style manual and the print room**
*0.0 · solver_dispute · F1 0.85 · novelty 0.565*
Best prose in the medium tier. Wedding-invitation-vs-census is the sharpest analogy in the corpus, and the asymmetry move (only one side carries institutional force, so the usual "both are right" retirement is unavailable) is a real CAT-style trap. Prescriptivism/descriptivism is also a canonical CAT topic. **Flagged solver_dispute — resolve the key before ship** (see risk note below).

**9. RC-MEDIUM-260822-0037 — institutional apologies and restitution**
*8.2 · needs_review · F1 0.725 · novelty 0.629*
Highest novelty in the medium tier, and the only necessary-but-not-sufficient normative essay — no scene, no case, pure moral reasoning, which CAT does run and this corpus otherwise doesn't. The watchmaker analogy and "difficulty is a bad solvent" carry it. **Weakest of the ten on craft** (F1 0.725; a few flabby meta-sentences, e.g. "The apparent subject is the fit between public words and restitution" — cut that line). Kept on distinctness; if you want to swap one out, this is the one.

**10. RC-MEDIUM-260822-0039 — Keep America Beautiful**
*8.2 · needs_review · F1 0.95 · novelty 0.561*
Visible-defeat-concealed-a-success-of-classification is a clean, teachable reversal, and the observatory-technician analogy at the close is the best image in the medium tier. Historically anchored, non-partisan, high compliance. Second permitted recantation opener.

## Risk notes before ship

1. **Two picks carry `solver_dispute` (score 0.0): M-0034.** That flag means a solver disagreed with the key, not that the passage is bad — but it must be adjudicated before shipping. M-0034's likeliest friction point is Q3 (B) vs Q8 (A), which both turn on institutional force; verify a solver can't read Q3 (D) as also complicating the asymmetry.
2. **Six of the ten are `needs_review`.** Only H-0052, H-0059 and E-0053 are approved.
3. **Screen column is uninformative.** Per the manifest, the similarity screen only went live with the OpenAI sets, so every `red` here (E-0061, M-0037, M-0039) was screened while every Claude set was not. Red ≠ worse; blank ≠ passed. Four of my ten are red and I did not treat that as a signal.
4. **The `— no: more precisely,` construction** survives in M-0033, M-0034, M-0037-adjacent prose and M-0039. Four of ten sharing a verbatim rhetorical hinge is visible to anyone who sits three papers in a row. Strip or vary it.
5. **Tier calibration.** M-0033 is conceptually the hardest medium here — arguably a hard. If your tier bands are load-bearing for scoring, re-check it.
